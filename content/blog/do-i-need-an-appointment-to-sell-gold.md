No — you never need an appointment to sell gold, silver, coins, or jewelry at The Gold Store. Walk in anytime during business hours and we'll evaluate your items on the spot.

## Why we don't do appointments

Most people who come in have no idea what a piece is actually worth until it's tested, which makes scheduling ahead of time pretty pointless — you'd be booking a slot for something neither of us can plan around. Walk-ins let you come in whenever it's convenient for you, whether that's during a lunch break, right after work, or on a Saturday morning.

## What to expect if you walk in right now

Evaluations typically take under 30 minutes for most jewelry, coins, or small collections. You'll get tested purity and weight, a transparent offer based on real-time market pricing, and — if you decide to sell — cash the same day.

## What to bring

Just the items you're considering selling and a valid government-issued photo ID. North Carolina law requires ID for any precious metals transaction, so that part isn't optional, but everything else is genuinely as simple as walking through the door.

## Is there ever a wait?

Occasionally, if we're mid-evaluation with another customer, you might wait a few minutes — but there's no scheduling system to navigate, no phone tag, no "next available slot." You show up, you're helped.
