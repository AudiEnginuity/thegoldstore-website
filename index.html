FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment to sell my items?

No — we welcome walk-ins during all business hours. Just bring your items and a valid photo ID.

How do you determine the price you'll pay?

We test purity and weight using professional equipment, then price against real-time precious metals market rates — the same rates shown in our ticker above.

What should I bring with me?

Bring the items you'd like to sell along with a valid government-issued photo ID, as required by North Carolina law for precious metals transactions.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. As a licensed and insured buyer, we follow all state reporting requirements while keeping your personal information secure.

Do you buy broken or damaged jewelry?

Absolutely. Broken chains, mismatched earrings, and damaged pieces are all evaluated on their metal content and value.
